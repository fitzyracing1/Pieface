THE VISION

Reclaim the
full stack.

Modern mobile operating systems have become extraordinarily complex. Pie Face takes the opposite path.

It is a new mobile OS designed to be developed, understood, and extended by individuals or small teams — entirely on a phone using Termux and Linux environments.

The goal is not feature parity with Android or iOS.
The goal is ownership, clarity, and speed of iteration.

CORE PHILOSOPHY

Design Principles

Phone-Native

The entire OS — kernel exploration, userspace, build system, and debugging — is designed to be developed on the device itself.

Extreme Minimalism

Target complexity and resource usage is roughly 1/10th that of mainstream mobile operating systems.

AI-Native

Built from the ground up to support local LLMs, agents, and on-device intelligence as first-class citizens.
