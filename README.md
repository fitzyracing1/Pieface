# Pie Face Mobile OS

> **A radically lightweight, phone-native mobile operating system.**  
> Built from scratch with ~1/10th the complexity of Android or iOS.

**Status:** Early Development  
**License:** MIT (core) + Custom for certain components  
**Website:** https://fitzyracing1.github.io/Pieface  
**Philosophy:** Extreme minimalism, full development on-device, maximum efficiency

---

## Vision

**Pie Face** is a new mobile operating system designed to be developed, debugged, and extended entirely on a phone using Termux + proot-distro Linux environments.

While modern mobile OSes have become massively complex, Pie Face takes the opposite approach:

- Minimal kernel and userspace footprint
- Designed for rapid iteration by solo builders and small teams
- First-class support for AI agents, local inference, and low-resource hardware
- Built with the same philosophy as the rest of the Earthenware Computer ecosystem

The goal is not to compete with Android or iOS feature-for-feature, but to create a clean, understandable, and extensible foundation that a single motivated developer can truly own and evolve.

---

## Key Features & Design Principles

- **Phone-First Development** — Full OS development, building, and debugging happens on the device itself
- **Extreme Minimalism** — Target ~1/10th the complexity and resource usage of mainstream mobile OSes
- **AI-Native Architecture** — Designed from the ground up for local LLMs, agents, and on-device intelligence
- **Modular & Hackable** — Clean separation of concerns with simple, well-documented interfaces
- **Low-Resource Friendly** — Runs well on older or constrained hardware
- **Ecosystem Integration** — Native support for 3XB, Loopa safety systems, Fire Fire Coin, and future quantum-adjacent components

## Why Pie Face?

Most mobile operating systems today are too large and complex for individuals or small teams to meaningfully contribute to or understand. Pie Face exists as a deliberate counter-movement:

- Reclaim ownership of the full software stack
- Enable true rapid prototyping at the OS level
- Create a platform optimized for the next wave of on-device AI and agentic computing
- Serve as a creative and practical foundation for ambitious builders
